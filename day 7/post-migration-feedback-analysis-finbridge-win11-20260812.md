## Top 3 Themes to Act on Today

> **Ranking method:** Severity first, then volume within severity tier. A single Blocker outranks any number of Minor or Friction comments. All five Blocker themes are ranked by volume as a tiebreaker. Friction and Minor themes do not feature in the top 3.

---

### Rank 1 — Account Lockouts | Count: 7 | Severity: Blocker

**Why it ranks here:**
It is the highest-volume Blocker, affecting 7 individuals, and the pattern is recurring — multiple users report being locked out two or three times in a single week, including immediately before client-facing activity. This is not a one-off; it indicates a systemic policy or configuration issue introduced by the migration. Volume within the Blocker tier places it first.

**Manager summary:**
Seven FinBridge staff have been locked out of their accounts since the migration, several repeatedly, pointing to a likely authentication or Intune policy misconfiguration that needs same-day investigation.

---

### Rank 2 — Floor 3 Printer Not Mapping | Count: 6 | Severity: Blocker

**Why it ranks here:**
Six comments, spanning multiple days and escalating in frustration ("team has given up and is walking to floor 2"), confirms this is a persistent, team-wide failure rather than isolated incidents. The impact is collective — one unresolved mapping issue is blocking an entire floor from printing client documents. It ranks second by volume within the Blocker tier and is overdue for resolution based on user feedback.

**Manager summary:**
The Floor 3 printer has failed to map automatically for at least three consecutive days post-migration, forcing the entire team to use a different floor and creating a client-facing risk with a deadline of 2pm today.

---

### Rank 3 — OneDrive Files Missing | Count: 4 | Severity: Blocker

**Why it ranks here:**
Four users report files that are absent from OneDrive with at least two citing same-day deadlines (a client meeting and the Q1 report). Data availability is a higher-risk concern than connectivity issues because it raises the possibility of data loss during migration — which may require immediate triage to determine whether files are missing, unsynced, or simply not visible. It ranks third over VPN Instability (also 4 comments) because potential data loss carries greater risk and recovery complexity.

**Manager summary:**
Four users cannot locate their OneDrive files post-migration, with at least two citing imminent deadlines today, and we need to confirm urgently whether this is a sync failure or data loss before end of morning.
