# Leadership Update — FinBridge Windows 11 Migration
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Prepared by:** DWP Analyst
**Audience:** Senior Leadership / Non-Technical Management

---

The Windows 11 migration for FinBridge staff is broadly complete and the majority of colleagues are now working normally on the new platform. In the first week, a number of staff experienced disruption — including account lockouts, slow login times, missing files, and an inability to access shared drives or connect reliably over VPN. These issues were real and affected people's ability to do their jobs. However, by the second week all of those problems had been resolved. Staff feedback has shifted noticeably from frustration to satisfaction, with many colleagues actively commenting on the improved speed and reliability of their new machines. The overall picture is one of a migration that caused a difficult first week but recovered well.

Two issues remain open and need attention. The floor 3 printer has not mapped automatically since the migration began and is now entering its third week unresolved — the team on that floor has resorted to walking to another floor to print, and frustration is high enough that staff are raising it with their own managers. This needs a direct fix or replacement decision this week. Separately, a configuration change applied to finance laptops on 4 August caused login times to roughly double — from under 20 seconds to over 40 seconds — and the problem has persisted every day since. We have identified the most likely cause as the compliance script added as part of that change, and a targeted check is under way to confirm this before any adjustment is made. There is also a newly emerging issue with Excel crashing when opening large files on the new machines, reported by nine finance staff. This has been logged and is being investigated as a priority given the finance team's reliance on large spreadsheets.
