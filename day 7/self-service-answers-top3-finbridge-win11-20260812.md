# Self-Service Answers — Top 3 Issues After Windows 11 Migration
**For FinBridge staff | Updated: 2026-08-12**

---

## 1. My account is locked and I can't log in

Wait 15 minutes and try logging in again — accounts unlock automatically after a short time. When you try again, type your password carefully and avoid clicking "sign in" more than once, as extra attempts can re-lock the account.

If you are still locked out after waiting, or this has happened more than once this week:
**Contact the Service Desk** and tell them: your name, your username, the time you were locked out, and how many times it has happened this week. This helps them spot a pattern and fix it faster.

---

## 2. The printer on Floor 3 is not showing up

Try disconnecting and reconnecting to the network. On your keyboard press **Windows key + L** to lock your screen, then log back in — this can trigger printers to map again.

If the printer still does not appear after logging back in:
**Contact the Service Desk** and tell them: your name, your floor and room number, the name of the printer if you know it, and how many days the issue has been happening. If your whole team is affected, mention that too so it is treated as a priority.

---

## 3. My files are missing from OneDrive

Look in the bottom-right corner of your screen for the OneDrive cloud icon. If it has a red cross or yellow warning on it, click it and select **Resume syncing** or **Fix issue**. Then wait a few minutes and check if your files reappear.

If your files are still missing after syncing, do not save anything new over the top of existing files:
**Contact the Service Desk immediately** and tell them: your name, the names of any files or folders you cannot find, when you last saw them, and whether you have a deadline today. The sooner you report it, the easier it is to recover.
