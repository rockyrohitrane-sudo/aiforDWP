# Self-Service Answers — Top 3 Post-Migration Issues
**FinBridge Staff | 2026-08-12**

---

## 1. Cannot Access the Credentials Vault

Sign out of the vault completely, then sign back in using your new Windows 11 login details — the migration may have changed which account it recognises. If that does not work, try opening the vault in a different browser (Edge or Chrome).

**Still not working? Contact the service desk.**
Tell them: your name, the exact error message on screen (a screenshot helps), and how many days it has been happening.

---

## 2. Locked Out of the Admin Console

Clear your browser's saved passwords for the console, then try logging in again with your current details. If you are fully locked out and cannot get past the login screen, stop trying — repeated failed attempts can extend the lockout.

**Contact the service desk straight away.**
Tell them: your name, your job role, and the name of the console you cannot reach.

---

## 3. Cannot Connect to Your Test Machines

Restart your computer fully (not just sleep or lock), then try again — the migration sometimes needs a clean restart to apply properly. Before reconnecting, make sure your VPN is switched on first.

**Still blocked? Contact the service desk.**
Tell them: your name, which machine you are trying to reach, the error message shown, and how many days it has been happening.
