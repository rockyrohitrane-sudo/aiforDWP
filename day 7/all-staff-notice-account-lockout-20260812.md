# All-Staff Notice — Account Sign-In Issue
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**From:** IT Service Desk
**To:** All FinBridge Staff

---

We are aware that a number of colleagues are experiencing difficulty signing in to their accounts this morning, including some accounts being locked out unexpectedly. We are sorry for the disruption and want you to know this is our top priority right now.

**If your account is locked, please try this first:**
Wait 15 minutes, then try signing in again. Type your password carefully and avoid clicking sign in more than once, as extra attempts can extend the lockout.

If you are still unable to sign in after waiting, please contact the Service Desk and have the following ready: your name, your username, the time the lockout happened, and how many times it has occurred today.

**What happens next:**
Our team is actively investigating and we will send a further update by **12:00 today** with progress, even if we do not yet have a full resolution.

Please do not raise multiple tickets for the same issue — this helps us keep the queue clear and respond faster.

Thank you for your patience.

**IT Service Desk**

*If your situation is urgent — for example, you have a client meeting or deadline — please call the Service Desk directly rather than emailing.*
