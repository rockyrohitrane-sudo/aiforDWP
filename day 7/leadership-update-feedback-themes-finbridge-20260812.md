# Leadership Update — End-User Feedback Review, FinBridge Win 11 Migration
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Prepared by:** DWP Analyst
**Audience:** Non-Technical Management

---

The Windows 11 migration has landed positively for a meaningful share of FinBridge staff. Several colleagues have commented that the rollout felt smoother than previous ones, and early feedback on the refreshed interface has been largely welcoming — in particular the improved visual design and the move to dark mode support. This indicates the migration itself was well-executed and that for users without access issues, day-to-day working is unaffected or improved.

However, three issues are preventing a group of staff from doing their jobs entirely and need to be resolved today. First, the shared credentials vault has been inaccessible to a team of staff for at least three days; the issue has already been escalated by affected individuals and the whole team remains stopped. Second, the console used by our support engineers to manage and fix problems has started locking people out, and this is spreading — what began as one engineer is now affecting the whole team. If this is not resolved quickly, our ability to support all other users will be compromised. Third, at least one engineer has been unable to reach their test environment for consecutive days, preventing them from carrying out their normal work. Self-service guidance has been issued to affected staff, and the service desk is prioritising all three as same-day issues. Progress updates will follow as each is resolved.
