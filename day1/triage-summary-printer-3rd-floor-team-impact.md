# Structured Triage Summary

## Summary (one line)
User reports the main printer on the 3rd floor is unavailable and the whole team is affected ahead of a client meeting at 2.

## Impact (who/how many/business urgency)
- Affected users: whole team (exact number to confirm).
- Scope: shared 3rd-floor printer outage affecting multiple users.
- Business urgency: time-critical due to client meeting at 2 (to confirm).

## known facts
- Reported issue: "printer gone" (printer unavailable).
- Location reference: "the big one on 3rd floor".
- User states whole team is affected.
- User states there is a client meeting at 2.

## Missing information to gather
- Exact printer name/queue name and asset ID.
- Whether printer is offline, missing from devices list, or showing an error state.
- Whether printing fails for all documents or specific jobs only.
- Exact number of affected users.
- Whether nearby alternative printers are available and working.
- Any visible printer panel error code/message.
- Time issue first noticed.
- Whether recent network/power/floor maintenance occurred (to confirm).

## likely catagory
- Shared printer service interruption (to confirm).
- Possible routing category: "Print Services/Shared Printer" (to confirm).

## Suggest first diagnostic step
Confirm outage scope and service state immediately by checking whether the identified 3rd-floor shared printer is online in print management and by submitting a single test print from a second user account; capture printer status/error message and timestamp for rapid escalation if offline.
