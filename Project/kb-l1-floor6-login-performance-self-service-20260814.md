# KB: Floor 6 — Login and Sign-In Delays (Self-Service)
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**For:** Floor 6 users  

---

## What Happened?

Friday's software update caused login and sign-in delays for some Floor 6 users. This wasn't a normal glitch—it was caused by how the new software interacted with your devices.

---

## How We Fixed It

IT reversed Friday's software change. We removed that deployment from Floor 6 devices, which should restore normal login speed.

---

## What to Do Now

1. **Log out completely** (if currently logged in):
   - Click **Start** → **Sign out**
   - Wait 1–2 minutes

2. **Log back in:**
   - Enter your credentials normally
   - Sign-in should be fast (within the normal ~30–45 seconds, or your usual time)

3. **If you're already signed in:**
   - You can keep working
   - Next time you log in, it should be fast

---

## If Problems Continue

**Still slow or can't sign in after 1 hour?**
- Restart your device (normal restart, not shutdown)
- Try logging in again
- If still failing, contact the Service Desk immediately with your device name

---

## FAQ

**Q: Will I lose my files or settings?**  
A: No. Only the problematic software is being removed. Your files and settings stay.

**Q: Do I need to do anything special?**  
A: Just log out and back in. The system handles the rest automatically.

**Q: Why did this happen?**  
A: The new software wasn't thoroughly tested on Floor 6's Windows 11 devices. We caught it, fixed it, and are adding better testing for the future.

---

**Questions?** Contact the Service Desk.
