# KB: Floor 6 — Missing Desktop Shortcuts (Self-Service)
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**For:** Floor 6 users  

---

## What Happened?

Friday's software installation accidentally removed desktop shortcuts on some Floor 6 computers. Your applications still work fine—only the quick-access shortcuts disappeared.

---

## How We're Fixing It

IT is deploying an automatic fix right now. **You don't need to do anything.** The shortcuts will restore themselves over the next hour.

---

## What to Expect

- **Shortcuts reappear on your desktop automatically.** No action required.
- If you see them after 1 hour, you're all set.
- If they're still missing after 1 hour, try one of these:

### Option 1: Trigger a Manual Sync (Fastest)
1. Click **Settings** (bottom left)
2. Go to **Accounts** → **Access work or school**
3. Select your work account → **Sync**
4. Wait 15 minutes; shortcuts should appear

### Option 2: Restart Your Device
- Restart your computer (standard restart, not shutdown)
- Shortcuts appear when you log back in

### Option 3: Contact IT
- If shortcuts still don't appear after 1 hour, contact the Service Desk
- Shortcuts not reappearing = priority issue; we'll fix it directly

---

## FAQ

**Q: Will I lose my other desktop files or settings?**  
A: No. Only shortcuts are affected. Everything else stays the same.

**Q: Do my applications still work?**  
A: Yes. Apps work perfectly. Only the desktop shortcuts are missing.

**Q: How long will the fix take?**  
A: Automatic restoration takes 1–2 hours. Manual sync speeds this up to 15 minutes.
