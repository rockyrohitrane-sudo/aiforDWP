## Floor 6 Desktop Shortcuts Update
**Date: 14 August 2026**

### What Happened

On Friday, Floor 6 received a new document management application as part of a software update. After installation, we discovered that desktop shortcuts (the small icons that help users quickly open their applications) have gone missing from some devices on Floor 6.

We have identified that the way the new software installed itself appears to have removed these shortcuts. The underlying applications are still working fine — only the quick-access icons on the desktop are affected.

### What's Being Done

We're deploying an automatic fix to restore the missing shortcuts across all affected Floor 6 devices. This restoration will happen overnight without any action required from users. Here's what's happening:

- We have halted the original Friday software installation to prevent further issues
- An automatic recovery process is being rolled out to bring shortcuts back to all desktops
- Each device will receive and install this fix when it connects to the network

Users should see their shortcuts reappear within the next 1–2 hours. If a restart is needed, they can manually restart their device or trigger a network sync through their device settings.

### What's Still Open

We're currently working to confirm:

- The exact cause of the shortcut removal (to prevent this happening again)
- A timeline for when 100% of Floor 6 devices will have shortcuts fully restored
- Whether the Friday software update will be re-released in a corrected version before general rollout

We will provide a full confirmation message to all Floor 6 partners once all shortcuts have been verified as restored.

---

**Support:** For questions or if shortcuts remain missing after 2 hours, please contact the Service Desk.
